## 3. Possible categories of data

Depending on the feature actually used and enabled, Performe AI may process:

- account and identity data, such as name, email, identifiers, and session status;
- profile and onboarding data, including date of birth, country, language, time zone, and preferences;
- personal goals;
- body, health, and fitness data, such as height, weight, body composition, metrics, and progress;
- food, meals, and nutritional information supplied or recorded;
- physical activity, workouts, and progress;
- routine, sleep, hydration, hunger, and habits;
- messages with Flow and free-form text;
- photos and audio selected by the user when media features are used;
- medication-, supplement-, and reminder-related information when that feature exists and is enabled;
- technical data for security, device management, authentication, requests, abuse prevention, and audits;
- notification, device, and preference data when notifications are enabled;
- entitlements, purchases, and subscription data if billing is introduced.

A type appearing in code or this list does not prove it is collected in every installation or environment. The final inventory must reflect actual configuration and data flows.

## 4. Sensitive personal data

Health, body, and other information that reveals or may reveal a health condition may be sensitive personal data. Processing requires enhanced controls, minimization, restricted access, and an appropriate legal basis. The classification of each flow and its definitive legal basis remain pending legal review.

## 5. Possible purposes

Data may be processed, to the extent needed, to create and protect an account; authenticate a user; personalize requested features; maintain history and progress; generate contextual Flow responses; process optional media; deliver enabled notifications; manage access rights; provide support; prevent fraud and abuse; ensure integrity; comply with obligations; establish or exercise rights; measure and improve security and reliability; and operate approved billing if introduced.

Each purpose must be confirmed against the actual inventory. Incompatible uses require a new assessment and, where applicable, new transparency or a new data-subject action.

## 6. Legal bases

Ordinary and sensitive data require distinct analyses. Depending on the flow and applicable law, performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps, compliance with legal or regulatory obligations, establishment or exercise of legal claims, consent where applicable, and other lawful grounds may be considered.

No ground is generically approved by this draft. Consent must not be used to obscure processing that relies on another ground and is not automatically the sole or best ground for all health-data processing.

**Final purpose-by-legal-basis matrix: pending legal review.**

## 7. Flow and artificial intelligence

Content sent to Flow may be processed by artificial intelligence systems needed for the requested response, with proportionate minimization and controls. The effective provider, model, region, retention, and subprocessors must be verified by environment before publication; an architectural reference alone does not confirm an active subprocessor.

Health data will not be used for behavioral advertising. This sentence is a proposed restriction requiring approval and verifiable implementation, not a certification of the current state of every environment. This draft does not make a generic “sale” or “no sale” claim before the final business model and contracts are legally assessed.

## 8. Photos and audio

When optional, photos and audio must be selected by the user and accompanied by a notice at the time of use. Media will be processed to perform the requested feature and must not be a condition for unrelated features. Device permissions, storage, derived data, providers, and retention must be explained according to the real flow.

## 9. Sharing and processors

Data may be shared with technology processors strictly to perform approved purposes under applicable instructions and controls; with authorities where an obligation or legal basis exists; and in corporate transactions only with the appropriate assessment, protection, and transparency. The final controller and processors have not been designated.

## 10. Processor and subprocessor inventory

The working register is in `processor-inventory.template.md`. Before effectiveness, it must be reconciled with the actual environment, contracts, DPA, regions, subprocessors, security measures, retention, and instructions. Architectural candidates are not legally approved processors.

## 11. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Brazil. Countries, regions, recipients, safeguards, and applicable mechanisms are **pending mapping and legal review**. Activation requires documentation and transparency consistent with the actual flow.

## 12. Retention and deletion

Data must be retained only as long as needed for approved purposes, legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, and the establishment or exercise of legal claims, and then deleted or anonymized where applicable. No numerical periods are approved in this draft. The working matrix is in `retention-matrix.template.md` and must cover active stores, backups, logs, media, derived data, and immutable legal-action records.

## 13. Security

As applicable to the effective architecture, proposed reasonable controls include authentication, TLS in transit, segregation, RLS and ownership checks, least privilege, server-side validation, limited and sanitized logs, audits, secret protection, idempotency, and monitoring. These controls must be verified by environment. No system is absolutely secure, and this Policy does not promise zero risk.

## 14. Data-subject rights

Under applicable law, data subjects may have rights to confirmation and access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion in applicable cases, portability subject to regulation, information about sharing, withdrawal of consent, objection, review of decisions, and petition before authorities. Scope, identity, and exceptions must be assessed for each request.

## 15. Exercising rights

Channel: `[PENDING — PRIVACY CONTACT CHANNEL]`. The procedure must include proportionate authentication, fraud protection, case tracking, routing, applicable timeframes, response, and evidence without collecting excessive data.

## 16. Withdrawal of consent

Where processing relies on consent, a data subject may withdraw it through an appropriate channel without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. If data are indispensable to an optional requested feature, withdrawal may make that feature unavailable. Processing retained under another valid basis requires separate transparency and analysis.

## 17. Account deletion

The operational deletion procedure remains pending. It must explain effects, authentication, records deleted or retained, backups, derived data, legal obligations, fraud prevention, and user confirmation, without promising instant deletion where a legitimate exception exists.

## 18. Automated decisions

Flow may assist in interpreting and presenting content, but Performe AI must not perform automated diagnosis. Any solely automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects requires an inventory, explanation, controls, applicable rights, and specific review. Official product states are defined by rules and the backend where applicable, not by Flow's prose.

## 19. Minors

The initial recommendation is to limit the beta to adults aged 18 or older. Minimum age, verification, countries, and any processing concerning minors require a formal decision. Until then, this draft does not authorize offering the service to minors.

## 20. Incidents

A procedure must exist and cover detection, containment, investigation, evidence preservation, risk assessment, notification of data subjects and authorities where applicable, and recurrence prevention. Owners, channels, criteria, and timeframes remain pending approval.

## 21. Changes and versioning

Material changes require a new version, date, approval record, and presentation where applicable. Historical actions remain linked to the exact version and bytes presented. This draft has no effective date and cannot be used as a current version.
