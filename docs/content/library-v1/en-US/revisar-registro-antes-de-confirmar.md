Every meal you log in Performe AI goes through a review step before it counts toward your day. The step exists because text, photos and audio can be read in more than one way, and only you know what you actually ate.

## How a log reaches review

You can describe the meal in text, take a photo, pick an image from your library, record audio or import an audio file of up to 3 minutes. The app turns it into a proposal with items and amounts. Nothing is saved to your day until you confirm.

Text works best with phrases made of a food and an amount, such as **150 g of rice** or **2 boiled eggs**. It does not understand every free-form sentence, so review carefully when the text is long or ambiguous.

## What to check before confirming

- **The right foods.** Make sure each item matches what you ate. Rice, beans and coffee, for example, come in very different versions.
- **The amounts.** Decimal amounts are kept exactly as you entered them. Fix anything that is off.
- **Approximate portions.** When an amount was estimated, the item is marked as approximate. Confirm or correct it.
- **Items without a calculation.** If the app could not calculate an item, it will not let you confirm the meal. Fix the item or remove it.
- **Zero is a value too.** Water or unsweetened coffee can have 0 kcal. That is a valid value, not an error.

## Photos of labels

If the photo shows a label, its values are used only when the reading is clear and complete. Even then, the app does not know how much you had: enter the amount.

## Edit without losing the rest

You can edit one item of the proposal without losing the others. If the connection fails and you try again, the same log is not saved twice.
