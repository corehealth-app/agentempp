## UX and governance guidance

- Present immediately before selection/upload or the first applicable processing.
- Do not require it in general onboarding when the photo/audio feature is not being used.
- Do not preselect it, bundle it with marketing, or create generic, perpetual consent for all future media.
- Explain the media type, concrete purpose, retention, derivatives, and processors when the real flow is approved.
- Allow cancellation without blocking features independent of the media.
- Present it again when the purpose, provider, retention, or processing changes materially, subject to legal review.
