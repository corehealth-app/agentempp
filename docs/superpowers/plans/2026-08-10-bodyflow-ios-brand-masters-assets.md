# BodyFlow iOS Brand Masters And Assets Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use
> `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (recommended) or
> `superpowers:executing-plans` to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use
> checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Reconstruct the approved BodyFlow identity from the sole authorized
JPEG source and deliver audited vector masters, deterministic raster exports,
an installable iOS asset catalog, and a human visual-approval checkpoint.

**Architecture:** The approved JPEG is committed as immutable provenance. One
outlined SVG master family is the source of every logo export; a pinned
build-time renderer creates the raster family and a manifest records source,
geometry, dimensions, color space, alpha policy, and checksums. SwiftUI consumes
typed asset names generated by Xcode; it never draws or approximates the logo at
runtime.

**Tech Stack:** Node.js 22+, pnpm 10.33.2, `sharp` 0.34.5 as a build-time tool,
SVG/PNG, Xcode 26.6, Swift 6, SwiftUI, iOS 18, Asset Catalogs, Swift Testing,
and XCUI visual evidence.

## Global Constraints

- The only visual authority is
  `PHOTO-2026-07-19-13-17-26.jpg`, `1491x1055`, SHA-256
  `af44d4b2036638720eaaf58c05fa6098f69b21c7639b91bb4a60bc85c64c15b7`.
- The product name is **BodyFlow**; no Balu, MPP, CoreFlow, or replacement name
  may enter the assets.
- Faithful visual equivalence at production sizes is required; mathematical
  identity with an unavailable original vector must not be claimed.
- The symbol preserves the flowing abstract `B`, forward arrow, teal highlight,
  coral lower stroke, soft-gold accent, and premium teal/charcoal treatment.
- The wordmark is outlined vector geometry. No font download, runtime font
  dependency, `<text>` SVG node, or generated text is permitted.
- No runtime SwiftUI logo drawing, path approximation, Canvas logo, or remote
  asset fetch is permitted.
- No third-party mark, competitor character, fake value, health claim, secret,
  PII, external URL, or credential may be embedded in an asset.
- The eight user-provided Cal AI onboarding montages are interaction references
  for Increment 4 only. They are not source artwork, are not committed to this
  repository, and do not alter the approved BodyFlow brand-master contract in
  this plan.
- The app remains app-first. No new architecture or asset depends on WhatsApp.
- The asset family contains no baked-in `pt-BR` or `en-US` product copy.
- This increment does not implement Android, screen redesign, localization,
  mascot animation, Rive, Lottie, live transport, migration, deploy, signing,
  TestFlight, or App Store submission.
- Mascot masters, coach illustrations, animation artboards, and the animation
  state manifest are assigned to the approved Motion And Illustration
  increment. This plan creates no temporary mascot presented as final.
- Every task uses RED/GREEN verification, `git diff --check`, focused review,
  and a separate logical commit.
- The human brand checkpoint is blocking: rejected candidates remain outside
  the production asset catalog.

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement the canonical contract and two-phase runner**

complete observed Sharp/native stack, configured concurrency/SIMD state, exact
installed Debian packages, and exact font paths/checksums. The Dockerfile uses
the recorded base-image digest and locked dependency/package versions.

review outputs only in a temporary repository copy, then runs the full brand
validator in that copy. Check mode compares the temporary trees recursively and
never promotes them. Write mode serializes runners with a repository lock,
revalidates the immutable snapshot, and swaps same-filesystem candidate
directories through a reversible transaction. A missing Docker runtime,
unavailable pinned package, identity mismatch, render/validation failure,
concurrent edit, promotion failure, or byte difference fails closed.

termination or host failure preserves the dead lock and transaction directories;
the next run refuses to continue until that recovery state is inspected.
