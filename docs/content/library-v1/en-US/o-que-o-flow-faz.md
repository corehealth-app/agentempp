Flow is the artificial intelligence assistant in Performe AI. It chats with you in text inside the app and helps you understand your data and your plans. Knowing what it does and does not do helps you use the conversation safely.

## What Flow does

- **Reads your official data.** It can read your progress, your metrics, a period summary and your reassessment details to answer your questions.
- **Proposes plans.** It can create a proposed workout plan or meal plan. The proposal shows up in the app as a draft.
- **Explains.** It can explain numbers, terms and next steps in plain language.

## What Flow does not do

- **It does not confirm anything for you.** A proposal becomes your plan only after you review and confirm it in the app. A chat message does not change your health data.
- **It does not recommend medication, supplements or doses.**
- **It does not diagnose and does not replace a professional.** It is not a doctor, dietitian or psychologist.
- **It does not handle emergencies.** If you have serious symptoms, seek medical care right away.

## It can be wrong

Flow is generated by artificial intelligence and can make mistakes or misread a question. Check important information and, when in doubt, talk to the professional who follows your care.

## Reviewing a proposal

When you open a plan draft, check each item. Uncertain fields are marked. You can edit, confirm or discard the proposal. If you imported a plan from a photo or PDF, the original document is kept with the draft.
