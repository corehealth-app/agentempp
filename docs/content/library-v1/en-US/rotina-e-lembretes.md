The Today tab gathers the items that are part of your day in the program, such as meals, hydration, workouts and supplement or medication items recorded in your routine. Reminders help you remember what you already planned.

## How reminders work

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone itself, from the times in your routine and the time zone on your account. The app schedules a limited number of notices at a time and renews the schedule when it syncs again.

To get reminders, allow Performe AI notifications when the app asks. You can change this permission later in iPhone Settings.

## Privacy on the lock screen

What appears on the lock screen follows your account's privacy preference. In the most private mode, the notice shows neither the item name nor the dose.

## Recording what happened

When you open a routine item, you can mark it as done, snooze it or skip it. Snoozing offers 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or a time you choose. The history shows what was recorded each day.

Reminders never change the time or dose of anything. They only tell you about what is already in your routine.

## When you sign out

When you sign out, the app removes the reminders that were scheduled on this device.
